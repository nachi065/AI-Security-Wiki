    Standards, regulation and official guidance

    [1] NIST. (2025, March). Adversarial Machine Learning: A Taxonomy and Terminology of Attacks and Mitigations (NIST AI 100-2e2025). https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/ai/nist.ai.100-2e2025.pdf Status: confirmed on NIST CSRC (published 20 March 2025).

    [2] NIST. (2024). Artificial Intelligence Risk Management Framework: Generative Artificial Intelligence Profile (NIST AI 600-1). https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework Status: not re-checked online in this revision; widely known; confirm the exact publication date and URL.

    [3] NIST. (2023). Artificial Intelligence Risk Management Framework (AI RMF 1.0) (NIST AI 100-1). https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework Status: not re-checked online in this revision; confirm URL.

    [4] ISO/IEC. (2023). ISO/IEC 42001:2023, Information technology: Artificial intelligence: Management system. https://www.iso.org/standard/81230.html Status: not re-checked online in this revision; confirm URL.

    [5] ISO/IEC. (2023). ISO/IEC 23894:2023, Information technology: Artificial intelligence: Guidance on risk management. https://www.iso.org/ Status: not re-checked online in this revision; confirm catalogue URL.

    [6] ISO/IEC. (2022). ISO/IEC 27001:2022, Information security, cybersecurity and privacy protection: Information security management systems: Requirements. https://www.iso.org/ Status: not re-checked online in this revision.

    [7] European Union. (2024). Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 laying down harmonised rules on artificial intelligence (Artificial Intelligence Act). Official Journal of the EU. https://eur-lex.europa.eu/ Status: instrument number not re-checked online in this revision; confirm the OJ reference and consolidated text on EUR-Lex.

    [8] European Union. (2026). Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 (the "AI Omnibus"), amending Regulation (EU) 2024/1689. As reported in: White & Case, EU AI Omnibus enters into force, amending the AI Act. https://www.whitecase.com/insight-alert/eu-ai-omnibus-enters-force-amending-ai-act Status: reported by one law-firm source (OJ publication 24 July 2026; entry into force 27 July 2026). Not checked against EUR-Lex. Corroborating coverage exists (e.g., Gibson Dunn, 27 May 2026, on the provisional agreement). VERIFY ON EUR-LEX.

    [9] ENISA. (2020, 15 December). Artificial Intelligence Cybersecurity Challenges. https://www.enisa.europa.eu/publications/artificial-intelligence-cybersecurity-challenges Status: confirmed on ENISA site.

    [10] ENISA. (2023, 7 June). Artificial Intelligence and Cybersecurity Research. https://enisa.europa.eu/publications/artificial-intelligence-and-cybersecurity-research Status: confirmed on ENISA site.
