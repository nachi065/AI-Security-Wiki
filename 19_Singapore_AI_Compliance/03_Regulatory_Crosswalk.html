No other primary text was read, and every other claim is tagged [Reported], [Recalled], [Conflict] or [Unverified] as explained on the section home page.     Legal force sorts each row under one of seven labels, so that law is not mistaken for guidance or for a voluntary framework. The label is this wiki's reading of the sources and carries the same verification caveat as the rest of the row.

    Label Meaning
    Binding law Enacted law or regulation that applies generally in the jurisdiction, including provisions that start on a later date.
    Binding in scope Binding only on a sector, a free zone, a state or province, or public bodies.
    Guidance Published by a government or regulator, and not binding in itself.
    Voluntary A standard, code or framework that an organization chooses to adopt.
    Proposed A bill, draft or consultation, or a proposal that lapsed. It is not law.
    Context A policy, authority, programme or summary row that sets no rule.
    Unclear The sources used do not settle whether it binds.

    MAS Guidelines on AI Risk Management: paragraph crosswalk

    MAS issued the Guidelines on Artificial Intelligence Risk Management on 7 October 2026. They set out MAS's supervisory expectations for financial institutions and replace the proposal that MAS consulted on in 2025. The table maps each group of paragraphs to wiki controls, test cases and templates. It was written from the published Guidelines, read on 10 October 2026, and the mapping is this wiki's own judgment.

    Point What the Guidelines say Paragraph
    Who it applies to All financial institutions, in proportion to their size and risk profile. For locally incorporated institutions under consolidated supervision, or that own critical information infrastructure, it applies on a group basis 1.2, 2.1
    What counts as AI Machine-based systems or models that derive outputs through learned premises. Tools whose outputs come only from predefined logic or rules are not AI for this purpose. Generative AI and AI agents are in scope 1.3, 1.5
    When it takes effect 7 October 2027 for Sections 3 and 4 (oversight; identification, inventory and risk materiality). 7 October 2028 for Sections 5 and 6 (life cycle controls; capability and capacity) 1.8
    Lighter route Basic governance policies and procedures are enough where poor performance or unavailability of the AI is unlikely to have a material adverse impact 2.3 to 2.5
    Related instruments The FEAT principles continue to apply. Institutions may refer to the CSA guidelines on securing AI systems, the IMDA Model AI Governance Framework and its agentic AI version, and AI Verify 1.2, footnotes 3 and 11

    Coverage shows how far the wiki covers the expectation: Direct (a wiki control addresses it) or Partial (the last column says what is missing).

    Paragraphs Expectation Wiki controls Test cases and templates Coverage What the wiki does not cover
    1.6, 2.1 to 2.2 Apply the Guidelines in proportion to the institution's size and activities and to the risk materiality of each AI use case AI-CTRL-011, AI-CTRL-037 TC-L01-005, TC-L01-009, SG-T2 Direct
    2.3 to 2.5 Basic governance where an AI failure is unlikely to have a material impact: a named senior manager, permitted and prohibited uses, human review of outputs, an approved list of AI tools, staff education, compliance checks and periodic review AI-CTRL-012, AI-CTRL-010, AI-CTRL-001, AI-CTRL-044 TC-L02-001, TC-L02-023, TC-L04-001, TC-L03-038 Direct
    3.1 to 3.4 The board approves and reviews the governance approach for AI risk, addresses AI in the risk appetite framework and understands AI well enough to challenge AI-CTRL-012, AI-CTRL-037 TC-L02-005, TC-L02-009, TC-L02-024 Partial No control or test case covers board understanding of AI or quantitative risk appetite measures
    3.5 Senior management implements the framework, assigns roles across business lines and control functions, escalates AI incidents and threshold breaches, and resources AI risk management AI-CTRL-012, AI-CTRL-035, AI-CTRL-044 TC-L02-004, TC-L01-003, TC-L17-018 Direct
    1.5, 3.6 Local senior management stays accountable where a group framework is used AI-CTRL-012 TC-L02-004 Partial The wiki does not address group and local accountability
    4.2 to 4.4 Identify AI use consistently across the institution, including AI inside third-party services and shadow AI, under a designated control function AI-CTRL-001, AI-CTRL-011 TC-L01-008, TC-L04-001, TC-L05-002, TC-L06-003 Direct
    4.5 to 4.9 Keep an accurate AI inventory with key attributes, linked to other inventories, with agent-specific attributes where relevant AI-CTRL-011, AI-CTRL-006 TC-L01-001, TC-L01-004, TC-L06-002, SG-T2 Direct
    4.10 to 4.13 Assess the risk materiality of each use case on impact, complexity and reliance, before and after controls, with a control function approving the rating AI-CTRL-011, AI-CTRL-037 TC-L01-005, TC-L01-009, TC-L02-007, TC-L02-009, SG-T2 Direct
    5.3 Contingency plans and fallback for high-risk use cases, and tested activation of kill switches AI-CTRL-040, AI-CTRL-025 TC-L01-018, TC-L06-022, TC-L06-023 Direct
    5.4 Data management: fitness for purpose, representativeness, quality, classification, security, privacy, and auditability and lineage AI-CTRL-017, AI-CTRL-029, AI-CTRL-013 TC-L13-001, TC-L13-002, TC-L13-006, TC-L15-012, SG-T3 Direct
    5.5 to 5.6 Transparency and explainability matched to the use case and its audience, with customer notice and redress for high-impact decisions AI-CTRL-041, AI-CTRL-039 TC-L03-003, TC-L03-029, TC-L03-008 Direct
    5.7 to 5.8 Define fair outcomes, run fairness assessments that consider protected attributes and their proxies, and document mitigation AI-CTRL-041 TC-L03-031, TC-L03-032 Direct
    5.9 Human oversight: assigned roles, competent overseers with authority to intervene, design that supports oversight, and review of interventions and near misses AI-CTRL-045, AI-CTRL-023 TC-L01-012, TC-L03-040, TC-L03-041 Direct
    5.10 to 5.11 Third-party AI: test it in the institution's own context, secure contractual visibility of changes, assess the supply chain and concentration risk, plan for failure and keep a right to audit AI-CTRL-014, AI-CTRL-031, AI-CTRL-040 TC-L16-008, TC-L16-010, TC-L16-011, TC-L16-012, TC-L16-023, TC-L16-025, TC-L03-042 Direct
    5.12 to 5.13 Document why an AI system, model or feature was selected over simpler alternatives AI-CTRL-033 TC-L01-002 Partial No control covers the rationale for selecting a model or feature
    5.14 to 5.15 Evaluation and testing against measurable thresholds, including stress, adversarial and sub-population tests; guardrails for known limitations; failure modes of generative AI and AI agents AI-CTRL-034, AI-CTRL-039, AI-CTRL-041 TC-L12-016, TC-L12-017, TC-L13-010, TC-D08-006, TC-D08-019 Partial Overfitting mitigation and statistical validation methods are outside the wiki
    5.16 Technology and cybersecurity controls: secured environments, access control and separation of duties, and governance of third-party components AI-CTRL-032, AI-CTRL-016, AI-CTRL-005, AI-CTRL-024 TC-L15-004, TC-L15-006, TC-L12-013, TC-L13-024, TC-L16-015 Direct
    5.17 Document development so that an independent party can understand and reproduce it AI-CTRL-030, AI-CTRL-038 TC-L13-013, TC-L14-019 Direct
    5.18 to 5.21 Review before deployment by people not involved in development, formal independent validation for high-risk use cases, and findings reported to the approval body AI-CTRL-033, AI-CTRL-038 TC-L01-002, TC-L02-013, TC-L14-002 Partial The wiki gates release on a security review. It has no control on independent model validation
    5.22 Technology and cybersecurity review before go-live: secure design, vulnerability assessment, penetration testing, red teaming and tests against adversarial threats AI-CTRL-034, AI-CTRL-033, AI-CTRL-032 TC-D08-002, TC-D08-020, TC-L14-002 Direct
    5.23 Ongoing monitoring with metrics and thresholds, drift checks, AI incident and issue management, user feedback, and logging of prompts and responses for generative AI and agents AI-CTRL-009, AI-CTRL-035, AI-CTRL-008 TC-L14-016, TC-L17-018, TC-L06-028, TC-D12-001 Direct
    5.24 Re-validation and review, regular for high-risk use cases and when triggered by changes, alerts or new risks AI-CTRL-034, AI-CTRL-038 TC-L12-018, TC-L01-015, TC-D08-021 Direct
    5.25 Change management: a way to judge how significant a change is, re-approval, version control with rollback, and tighter controls for automatic updates AI-CTRL-033, AI-CTRL-030 TC-L06-034, TC-L14-012, TC-L14-013, TC-L01-015 Direct
    5.26 Retirement and decommissioning of AI AI-CTRL-011, AI-CTRL-028 TC-L01-019, TC-L12-024, TC-L06-035 Direct
    6.1 to 6.2 Competence and conduct of the people who develop, deploy and use AI, with regular review of capability and capacity AI-CTRL-044 TC-L03-038, TC-L02-022 Direct
    6.3 Technology infrastructure adequate for performance, scalability and resilience AI-CTRL-032, AI-CTRL-040 TC-L15-002, TC-L15-019 Direct

    Of the 26 groups of paragraphs, 21 are a direct match and 5 are partial. The partial ones are where the Guidelines go into model risk management and board governance, which sit outside a security and governance wiki: board understanding of AI and risk appetite measures, accountability between a group and its Singapore entity, the rationale for selecting a model, statistical validation methods and independent model validation.
