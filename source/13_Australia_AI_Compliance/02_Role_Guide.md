# Australia: Role Guide

> **Verification required.** Claims on this page about Australian law were compiled on 7 October 2026 from secondary web sources and from memory of the law. No primary legal text was read, and each claim is tagged [Reported], [Recalled], [Conflict] or [Unverified] as explained on the [section home page](index.md). Check the primary text before relying on any of them. This is not legal advice.

Use the [Australia AI Regulatory Hub](01_Australia_AI_Regulatory_Hub.md) for instrument detail, and the templates in this section.

## AI practitioners

For engineers, data scientists and security staff.

- Record which automated decisions significantly affect people, and the data and automation level each one uses ([AU-T1](AU-T1_ADM_Transparency_Register.md)).
- Provide explanations that match your automated decision-making (ADM) statement.
- Control shadow AI. Staff using personal accounts is a reported risk.

## Product companies

For vendors, SaaS providers and platforms.

- Give customers the facts they need for their own ADM statements.
- Avoid misleading claims about AI capability.
- Expect APRA-regulated customers to pass CPS 230 and CPS 234 duties down to you.

## Auditors

For internal, external and assurance auditors.

- Test that ADM statements match the actual systems.
- Check shadow AI controls.
- Treat penalty claims as unverified until confirmed.

## Implementors

For programme leads, GRC teams, CISOs and DPOs.

- Build an ADM register before 10 December 2026.
- Update privacy policies and notices.
- Adopt the Guidance for AI Adoption practices as policy headings ([AU-T2](AU-T2_AI_Adoption_Self_Assessment.md)).

## Shared evidence list

- ADM register
- Privacy notices
- Shadow AI discovery
- Impact assessments
- Vendor due diligence
