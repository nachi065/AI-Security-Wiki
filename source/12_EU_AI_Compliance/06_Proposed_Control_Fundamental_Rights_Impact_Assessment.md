# EU Drivers for AI-CTRL-041 and AI-CTRL-042

> **How this relates.** [AI-CTRL-041](../03_Control_Library/03_AI_Security_Control_Objectives_Library.md#ai-ctrl-041) and [AI-CTRL-042](../03_Control_Library/03_AI_Security_Control_Objectives_Library.md#ai-ctrl-042) are part of the [AI Security Control Objectives Library](../03_Control_Library/03_AI_Security_Control_Objectives_Library.md). This page lists the EU instruments that call for them. The drivers are tagged [Recalled] or [Reported]; check the primary text before relying on them. This is not legal advice.

## EU drivers for AI-CTRL-042

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Control | [AI-CTRL-042](../03_Control_Library/03_AI_Security_Control_Objectives_Library.md#ai-ctrl-042) Fundamental Rights and Data Protection Impact Assessment |
| EU drivers | GDPR Art. 35 [Recalled]; AI Act Art. 27 FRIA for certain deployers [Recalled; scope to verify] |
| Template | [T02 Impact Assessment (FRIA and DPIA Combined)](T02_Impact_Assessment_FRIA_DPIA.md) |
| Test cases | [TC-L03-034](../10_Test_Case_Library/L03-legal-privacy-and-compliance.md#tc-l03-034), [TC-L03-010](../10_Test_Case_Library/L03-legal-privacy-and-compliance.md#tc-l03-010), [TC-L03-011](../10_Test_Case_Library/L03-legal-privacy-and-compliance.md#tc-l03-011) |
| Related controls | [AI-CTRL-013](../03_Control_Library/03_AI_Security_Control_Objectives_Library.md#ai-ctrl-013), [AI-CTRL-037](../03_Control_Library/03_AI_Security_Control_Objectives_Library.md#ai-ctrl-037) |

## EU drivers for AI-CTRL-041

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Control | [AI-CTRL-041](../03_Control_Library/03_AI_Security_Control_Objectives_Library.md#ai-ctrl-041) Fairness, Bias Testing and Explainability |
| EU drivers | AI Act data governance and bias examination (Art. 10) and the right to explanation (Art. 86) [Recalled]; GDPR Art. 22 [Recalled]; the strict-necessity standard for special category data, reported as retained by the omnibus [Reported] |
| EU addition to the evidence | Where special category data is needed for bias testing, keep the strict-necessity test and the safeguards on record. [TC-L03-032](../10_Test_Case_Library/L03-legal-privacy-and-compliance.md#tc-l03-032) tests for that record |
| Test cases | [TC-L03-031](../10_Test_Case_Library/L03-legal-privacy-and-compliance.md#tc-l03-031), [TC-L03-032](../10_Test_Case_Library/L03-legal-privacy-and-compliance.md#tc-l03-032), [TC-L03-008](../10_Test_Case_Library/L03-legal-privacy-and-compliance.md#tc-l03-008), [TC-L03-029](../10_Test_Case_Library/L03-legal-privacy-and-compliance.md#tc-l03-029) |

The GCC instruments behind AI-CTRL-041 are on the [GCC drivers page](../11_GCC_AI_Compliance/06_Proposed_Control_Fairness_Bias_Explainability.md).
