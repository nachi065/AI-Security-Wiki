# T06 Transparency Notice (AI Act Article 50 and GDPR)

> **Verification required.** This is a working template, not a regulator-approved form. Where it cites an article or a date, the citation comes from secondary sources or from memory of the legal text and is a pointer to check. This is not legal advice.

Wiki controls: [AI-CTRL-039](../03_Control_Library/03_AI_Security_Control_Objectives_Library.md#ai-ctrl-039), [AI-CTRL-013](../03_Control_Library/03_AI_Security_Control_Objectives_Library.md#ai-ctrl-013). Related cases: [TC-L03-003](../10_Test_Case_Library/L03-legal-privacy-and-compliance.md#tc-l03-003), [TC-L03-030](../10_Test_Case_Library/L03-legal-privacy-and-compliance.md#tc-l03-030). The Art. 50 duties are [Recalled], and the date question is [Conflict] ([hub section 2.2](01_EU_AI_Regulatory_Hub.md#22-reported-omnibus-changes)). The voluntary marking and labelling code (June 2026) is a reference and does not by itself give legal protection [Reported]. Translate the notice into the language of each Member State you serve, and have a native legal reviewer check each version.

## 1. Short notice (point of interaction)

> You are interacting with an AI system. It may make mistakes. [Company] uses it to [purpose]. You can ask for a person to review any decision that affects you. More information: [link].

## 3. AI Act transparency add-ons

| Case | Wording or action |
|---|---|
| Chatbot or interaction | Tell people they are interacting with AI, unless that is obvious |
| Synthetic audio, image, video, text | Machine-readable marking by the provider; detection support |
| Deepfake | The deployer discloses that the content is artificially generated or manipulated |
| AI-generated text on matters of public interest | Disclose, unless human review and editorial responsibility apply |
| Emotion recognition or biometric categorisation | Inform the persons exposed |
| Workplace high-risk system | Inform workers and their representatives before use |

Sample label: "This [image/audio/text] was generated or altered by AI."
