# AI Policy, Standards and Acceptable Use

> **Purpose:** Provide practical acceptable-use rules for AI users, developers, vendors, custom AI applications and agents.

> **Audience:** All AI users, developers, governance, compliance, HR/policy teams, security awareness.

## Acceptable AI Use

AI tools may be used only when approved, governed and aligned with data classification, information protection, confidentiality, security and regulatory requirements.

## Prohibited AI Use

Users must not submit or upload:

- Critical infrastructure information.
- Restricted or confidential documents unless approved controls are in place.
- Passwords, API keys, certificates, tokens or secrets.
- Customer, employee or regulated data unless explicitly approved.
- Internal architecture diagrams, network configurations or security procedures.
- Full proprietary source code or sensitive application logic unless approved.
- Procurement, legal, audit or security assessment data to unapproved AI platforms.

## Public AI Platform Rules

- Use approved enterprise AI platforms where available.
- Do not use personal AI accounts for business data.
- Do not bypass browser, endpoint or DLP controls.
- Treat AI output as unverified until reviewed.

## AI Coding Assistant Rules

- Do not submit production credentials or sensitive source code.
- Review AI-generated code under secure coding processes.
- Validate dependencies and generated configurations.
- Report suspicious or unsafe AI suggestions.

## Agentic AI Rules

- No autonomous action without defined owner, identity, scope, tool list, approval workflow, logs and kill switch.
- Sensitive business actions must require human approval.
- Tool/API permissions must follow least privilege.
