# South Korea: Role Guide

> **Verification required.** Claims on this page about South Korean law were compiled on 7 October 2026 from secondary web sources and from memory of the law. No primary legal text was read, and each claim is tagged [Reported], [Recalled], [Conflict] or [Unverified] as explained on the [section home page](index.md). Check the primary text before relying on any of them. This is not legal advice.

Use the [South Korea AI Regulatory Hub](01_South_Korea_AI_Regulatory_Hub.md) for instrument detail, and the templates in this section.

## AI practitioners

For engineers, data scientists and security staff.

- Add visible or audible markers and notices to generated media ([KR-T2](KR-T2_Generative_AI_Labelling_and_Notice.md)).
- Where a system is high-impact, document its purpose, data, impact on rights, oversight and explanation ([KR-T1](KR-T1_High_Impact_AI_Determination.md)).
- Keep logs and documentation ready for inspection.
- Test Korean-language behaviour and safeguards.

## Product companies

For vendors, SaaS providers and platforms.

- Check the domestic representative thresholds ([KR-T3](KR-T3_Domestic_Representative_Check.md)).
- Classify your product as generative, high-impact or high-performance.
- Prepare Korean-language notices and labels.
- Use the grace year to build evidence.

## Auditors

For internal, external and assurance auditors.

- State the basis: a binding Act with a grace period on fines, and criteria whose detail comes from the decree.
- Test labelling and notices on samples.
- Check the record of the high-impact determination.
- Verify that a representative has been appointed where the thresholds are met.

## Implementors

For programme leads, GRC teams, CISOs and DPOs.

- Add Korea to the regional applicability register.
- Set up a project to finish the documentation before the grace period ends.
- Assign a local representative and legal counsel where needed.
- Track the decree and the guidelines.

## Shared evidence list

- High-impact determination
- Impact assessments
- Label and notice test results
- Representative appointment
- Documentation and logs
