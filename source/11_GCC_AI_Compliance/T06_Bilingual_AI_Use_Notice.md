# T06 Bilingual AI Use Notice (Arabic and English)

> **Verification required.** This is a working template, not a regulator-approved form. Where it names a regional instrument, the claim comes from secondary sources and is a pointer to check, not a confirmed legal requirement. This is not legal advice.

<!-- -->

> **How to use:** This is a blank form. Copy it and fill in the empty cells and blanks for your system. To copy it, follow the "Suggest an edit to this page" link in the footer to reach its Markdown source.

Wiki cases: [TC-L03-003](../10_Test_Case_Library/L03-legal-privacy-and-compliance.md#tc-l03-003), [TC-L03-030](../10_Test_Case_Library/L03-legal-privacy-and-compliance.md#tc-l03-030). CBUAE is reported to expect Arabic and English disclosure for financial institutions [Reported]. Have a native Arabic legal reader review before publication. The Arabic below is a draft for that review, not a certified translation.

Fill the placeholders in [brackets]. Delete any paragraph that does not apply.

## 1. Short notice (shown at point of interaction)

**English**
You are interacting with an AI system. It may make mistakes. [Company] uses it to [purpose]. You can ask for a person to review any decision that affects you. Learn more: [link].

<p dir="rtl" lang="ar"><strong>العربية</strong><br>
أنت تتفاعل مع نظام ذكاء اصطناعي، وقد يرتكب أخطاء. تستخدمه [الشركة] من أجل [الغرض]. يمكنك طلب مراجعة بشرية لأي قرار يؤثر عليك. لمزيد من المعلومات: [الرابط].</p>

## 2. Full notice

### 2.1 What we use AI for

**English:** We use AI to [purpose]. The system [recommends / decides / drafts]. A person [reviews / does not review] the result before it affects you.

<p dir="rtl" lang="ar"><strong>العربية:</strong> نستخدم الذكاء الاصطناعي من أجل [الغرض]. يقوم النظام بـ [التوصية / اتخاذ القرار / الصياغة]. [يراجع / لا يراجع] شخص مختص النتيجة قبل أن تؤثر عليك.</p>

### 2.2 What data we use

**English:** We use [data categories]. We collect it from [sources]. We keep it for [period] in [country].

<p dir="rtl" lang="ar"><strong>العربية:</strong> نستخدم [فئات البيانات]. نجمعها من [المصادر]. نحتفظ بها لمدة [المدة] في [الدولة].</p>

### 2.3 Why we may process it

**English:** Our legal basis is [basis]. [If consent: you may withdraw consent at any time by [method].]

<p dir="rtl" lang="ar"><strong>العربية:</strong> الأساس النظامي للمعالجة هو [الأساس]. [في حال الموافقة: يمكنك سحب موافقتك في أي وقت عبر [الطريقة].]</p>

### 2.4 Your rights

**English:** You may ask to access, correct or delete your data, to object to processing, and to request human review of an automated decision. Contact [contact] or [channel]. We reply within [period].

<p dir="rtl" lang="ar"><strong>العربية:</strong> يحق لك طلب الاطلاع على بياناتك أو تصحيحها أو حذفها، والاعتراض على المعالجة، وطلب مراجعة بشرية لقرار آلي. يمكنك التواصل مع [جهة الاتصال] أو عبر [القناة]. نرد خلال [المدة].</p>

### 2.5 Sharing and transfers

**English:** We share data with [recipients]. [We transfer data to [country] under [mechanism].]

<p dir="rtl" lang="ar"><strong>العربية:</strong> نشارك البيانات مع [الجهات]. [ننقل البيانات إلى [الدولة] وفق [الآلية].]</p>

### 2.6 Limits of the system

**English:** The system may be wrong or biased. Do not rely on it alone for [high-stakes matters].

<p dir="rtl" lang="ar"><strong>العربية:</strong> قد يكون النظام خاطئاً أو متحيزاً. لا تعتمد عليه وحده في [الأمور ذات الأثر الكبير].</p>

### 2.7 Complaints

**English:** If you are not satisfied, contact [DPO]. You may also contact [regulator].

<p dir="rtl" lang="ar"><strong>العربية:</strong> إذا لم تكن راضياً، تواصل مع [مسؤول حماية البيانات]. ويمكنك كذلك التواصل مع [الجهة التنظيمية].</p>

## 3. Publication checklist

- [ ] Both languages show the same content and equal prominence
- [ ] Arabic reviewed by a native legal reader
- [ ] Right-to-left layout tested on web and mobile
- [ ] Notice reachable before first use
- [ ] Contact and regulator details confirmed from the primary source
- [ ] Version, date and owner recorded

## 4. Generative AI add-on

**English:** Content marked [AI-generated] was created with AI. Check important facts before relying on them.

<p dir="rtl" lang="ar"><strong>العربية:</strong> المحتوى المشار إليه بعبارة [مُنشأ بالذكاء الاصطناعي] أُنتج باستخدام الذكاء الاصطناعي. تحقق من المعلومات المهمة قبل الاعتماد عليها.<br>
