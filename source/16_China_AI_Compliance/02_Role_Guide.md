# China (Mainland): Role Guide

> **Verification required.** Claims on this page about Chinese law were compiled on 7 October 2026 from secondary web sources and from memory of the law. No primary legal text was read, and each claim is tagged [Reported], [Recalled], [Conflict] or [Unverified] as explained on the [section home page](index.md). Check the primary text before relying on any of them. This is not legal advice.

Use the [China AI Regulatory Hub](01_China_AI_Regulatory_Hub.md) for instrument detail, and the templates in this section.

## AI practitioners

For engineers, data scientists and security staff.

- Implement explicit and implicit labels on generated content, including metadata ([CN-T2](CN-T2_Content_Labelling_Implementation_Record.md)).
- Build content moderation and complaint handling for public-facing services.
- Keep records of training data provenance and legality.
- Separate the environments and data flows for mainland users, and plan for localisation and transfer rules.
- Add dependency safeguards and protections for minors to companion-style products.

## Product companies

For vendors, SaaS providers and platforms.

- Check before launch whether you need algorithm or generative AI service filings ([CN-T1](CN-T1_Filing_and_Registration_Readiness.md)).
- Expect local partners and platforms to demand labelling and compliance evidence.
- If you are a foreign vendor, confirm whether you operate a service in mainland China at all, and consult mainland counsel.
- Set up an ethics review process for high-risk functions ([CN-T3](CN-T3_Ethics_Review_Committee_Record.md)).
- Avoid claiming an approval or filing that you do not hold.

## Auditors

For internal, external and assurance auditors.

- Rely on mainland legal advice for the criteria, and state the basis in the report.
- Test for the presence of labels and metadata on a sample of generated content.
- Check the ethics review minutes and the external expert input.
- Inspect filing records, including updates made after a material change.
- Test complaint handling and content review logs.

## Implementors

For programme leads, GRC teams, CISOs and DPOs.

- Create a China overlay on the group standard, and keep it visibly separate from the group text.
- Assign a mainland compliance owner and a legal advisor.
- Maintain a tracker of measures (the [China AI Regulatory Hub](01_China_AI_Regulatory_Hub.md) is a starting point) and a filing calendar.
- Align data residency with business and legal constraints.
- Run labelling and content review tests every quarter.

## Shared evidence list

- Filing and assessment records
- Labelling test results
- Ethics committee minutes
- Content review and complaint logs
- Data classification and transfer records
- Training data provenance
