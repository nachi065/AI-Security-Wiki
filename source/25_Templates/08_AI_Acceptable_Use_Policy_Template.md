# AI Acceptable Use Policy Template (.docx)

**[Download the AI Acceptable Use Policy Template (.docx)](AI_Acceptable_Use_Policy_Template.docx)**

> **Purpose:** Give an organization a starting draft of an AI acceptable use policy that it can adapt, approve and publish, built from the [AI Policy, Standards and Acceptable Use](../08_Governance/17_AI_Policy_Standards_and_Acceptable_Use.md) page and the controls it points to.

> **Audience:** CISOs, AI governance committees, policy owners, legal, HR and compliance.

> **Before you rely on it:** This is a template, not legal advice. Every [highlighted placeholder] is a decision for your organization, such as approved tools, data classes and approvers. Replace each one and have the policy approved by the right owner before you publish it.

## How to use it

1. **Decide.** Work through the how-to-use box and agree each placeholder with its owner.
2. **Adapt.** Replace every placeholder, delete what does not apply and keep the source notes until review.
3. **Map.** Check Annex A against the controls you have implemented.
4. **Approve.** Have the governing body approve the policy, as the wiki's AI Governance Operating Model asks, and record it in the document control table.
5. **Publish.** Publish the policy with the quick reference card in Annex B, and collect the acknowledgement in Annex C.
6. **Review.** Review it on the cycle set in section 12, and when tools, laws or the controls change.
