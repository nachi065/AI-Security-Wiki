# AI Security Audit and Evidence Checklist Template (.xlsx)

**[Download the AI Security Audit and Evidence Checklist Template (.xlsx)](AI_Security_Audit_and_Evidence_Checklist.xlsx)**

> **Purpose:** Run an audit of AI security controls: test each of the {{n:controls}} wiki controls against its audit procedure, request and track evidence, log findings and exceptions, and prepare for ISO/IEC 42001 certification. Built from the [AI Security Audit and Evidence Checklist](../06_Testing_and_Assurance/14_AI_Security_Audit_and_Evidence_Checklist.md) page.

> **Audience:** Internal audit, GRC, assurance teams and control owners preparing for an audit.

> **Before you rely on it:** The GCC and EU checklists and evidence registers say their regional criteria come from secondary sources and must be checked. The regional selection, the due-soon window for evidence requests and the 30-day exception expiry warning are workbook defaults.

## How to use it

1. **Audit Plan.** Record the audit, scope and period, and choose the regional checklists.
2. **Evidence Requests.** Request evidence and record due and received dates. Status shows what is overdue.
3. **Control Tests.** For each control in scope, follow the audit test, record the sample size, result and evidence, and check the six evidence quality criteria.
4. **Regional Tests.** Work through the GCC and EU tests that apply.
5. **Findings and exceptions.** Raise a finding for every partially effective or ineffective result and enter its ID on the test row. Record approved exceptions in the register.
6. **Summary.** Use the totals, evidence status, findings and exceptions in the audit report.

## The result scale

Results use the wiki T08 scale: E (effective), P (partially effective, finding raised), I (ineffective, finding raised) and N (not tested, with the reason).
