# AI Security Programme Charter Template (.docx)

**[Download the AI Security Programme Charter Template (.docx)](AI_Security_Programme_Charter_Template.docx)**

> **Purpose:** Bring the programme together in one document that a governing body can approve. The wiki's material on governance, risk, controls, testing and regulation is spread over many pages; the charter records the decisions and links each one to the page that holds the detail.

> **Audience:** Accountable executives, AI governance leads, CISOs, risk and compliance leads.

> **Before you rely on it:** The example values in the charter (meeting frequencies, exception limits, response times, retention periods) are the wiki's illustrative defaults from [AI Operating Defaults](../08_Governance/18_AI_Operating_Defaults.md), not values from a standard or a law. Replace them with your own. This is not legal advice.

## How to use it

1. **Draft.** The accountable executive's team fills in sections 1 and 3 to 13 from the linked pages.
2. **Decide the reserved items.** The governing body approves the risk appetite and prohibited uses (section 2), the AI policy (section 5) and the decision rights for the items it keeps.
3. **Adopt the defaults.** Agree each value in section 7, or replace it, and record who approved it.
4. **Review.** Review the charter at least annually and after a material incident, and record the changes in section 13.
