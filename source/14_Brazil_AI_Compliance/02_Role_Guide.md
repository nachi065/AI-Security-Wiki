# Brazil: Role Guide

> **Verification required.** Claims on this page about Brazilian law were compiled on 7 October 2026 from secondary web sources and from memory of the law. No primary legal text was read, and each claim is tagged [Reported], [Recalled], [Conflict] or [Unverified] as explained on the [section home page](index.md). Check the primary text before relying on any of them. This is not legal advice.

Use the [Brazil AI Regulatory Hub](01_Brazil_AI_Regulatory_Hub.md) for instrument detail, and the templates in this section.

## AI practitioners

For engineers, data scientists and security staff.

- Keep a review route for decisions based on automated processing ([BR-T1](BR-T1_LGPD_Automated_Decision_Review_Record.md)).
- Record data lineage so that LGPD rights can be met.
- Keep your design flexible enough to take on tiered duties if the bill passes.

## Product companies

For vendors, SaaS providers and platforms.

- Do not claim compliance with a law that is not in force.
- Prepare LGPD documentation for Brazilian customers.
- Watch the Chamber for changes to the risk tiers.

## Auditors

For internal, external and assurance auditors.

- Use the LGPD and sector rules as criteria. The bill is a reference only.
- Test how requests to review automated decisions are handled.

## Implementors

For programme leads, GRC teams, CISOs and DPOs.

- Map systems to their likely tiers now ([BR-T2](BR-T2_PL2338_Readiness_Watchlist.md)).
- Assign an owner for the watchlist.
- Align the data protection programme with ANPD guidance.

## Shared evidence list

- LGPD records
- Review route logs
- Tier mapping
- Watchlist
