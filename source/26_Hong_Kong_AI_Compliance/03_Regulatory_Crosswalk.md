## HKMA guidance on responsible AI adoption: principle crosswalk

The HKMA issued its Supervisory Guidance for Responsible Adoption of Artificial Intelligence on 15 October 2026, as Annex A to a circular to authorized institutions. It supersedes the HKMA's High-level Principles on Artificial Intelligence of 1 November 2019 and groups nine principles under three pillars: Accountability (1 to 3), Resilience (4 to 6) and Trust (7 to 9). It was read on 25 October 2026, and the mapping below is this wiki's.

| Point | What the guidance says |
|---|---|
| Who it applies to | Authorized institutions, applying the principles proportionately and using existing governance where appropriate |
| When | No fixed date. Institutions should uplift their policies, procedures and controls in a timely manner |
| Read with | The HKMA report on practical insights from the second cohort of the GenAI Sandbox, and other supervisory requirements |
