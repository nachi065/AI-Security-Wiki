# ISO/IEC 42001 Crosswalk

> **Purpose:** Let a team that is building or auditing an AI management system under ISO/IEC 42001 find the wiki control objectives, evidence and test cases that support each Annex A control.

> **Audience:** AI management system owners, internal auditors, certification leads, GRC teams.

> **Using the two together:** Use ISO/IEC 42001 for the management system and its certification. Use this wiki for the security control objectives, evidence and tests that sit under the Annex A controls.

## About the standard and this page

ISO/IEC 42001:2023 sets requirements for an AI management system. Its Annex A lists 38 controls, numbered from A.2.2 to A.10.4, and its main clauses 4 to 10 hold the management system requirements. The standard is sold by ISO and its text is copyrighted.

The tables are generated each time the site is built. They are read from the ISO/IEC 42001 field of each control in the [AI Security Control Objectives Library](../03_Control_Library/03_AI_Security_Control_Objectives_Library.md). To change a mapping, edit the control; this page follows on the next build.

> **Verify before use.** Control numbers and titles are given as the control library cites them and were not checked against a copy of the standard for this page. The list of 38 Annex A numbers was written from the structure of the standard. Check every number and title against your own copy before quoting it in a statement of applicability or an audit. A wiki control citing an Annex A control means it supports that control, not that it satisfies it.

## How to read the tables

- **Wiki controls** are the control objectives whose record cites the Annex A control or clause.
- **Test cases under those controls** counts the distinct test cases that test at least one of those controls.
- An Annex A control with no name and "None" in the next column is not cited by any wiki control.
