**How to read the FINOS columns.** The cross-reference cites FINOS AI Governance Framework (AIGF) risk identifiers for traceability. **Match** shows how closely the FINOS risk fits: **Direct** (same risk), **Partial** (overlaps part of it) or **None seen** (no equivalent in the FINOS repository). The matching is this wiki's judgment, made from FINOS titles and the links between FINOS risks and mitigations. The reverse index, from each FINOS risk and mitigation to this wiki, is in the [FINOS AI Governance Framework Crosswalk](../23_Framework_Crosswalks/01_FINOS_AIGF_Crosswalk.md).
## Mapping Usage

Use this mapping during solution architecture review, vendor PoC scoring, audit evidence collection and recurring risk review. Each AI project should identify applicable risks and provide the mapped evidence before production approval.
