# CA-T1 Algorithmic Impact Assessment Worksheet

> **Verification required.** This is a working template, not a regulator-approved form. Where it names an instrument or a date, the claim comes from secondary sources or from memory of the law and is a pointer to check. This is not legal advice.

Wiki controls: [AI-CTRL-011](../03_Control_Library/03_AI_Security_Control_Objectives_Library.md#ai-ctrl-011), [AI-CTRL-037](../03_Control_Library/03_AI_Security_Control_Objectives_Library.md#ai-ctrl-037). The worksheet is modelled on the idea of the federal Algorithmic Impact Assessment [Reported]. It is a working draft; use the official tool for federal work.
