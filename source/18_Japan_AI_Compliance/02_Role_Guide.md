# Japan: Role Guide

> **Verification required.** Claims on this page about Japanese law were compiled on 7 October 2026 from secondary web sources and from memory of the law. No primary legal text was read, and each claim is tagged [Reported], [Recalled], [Conflict] or [Unverified] as explained on the [section home page](index.md). Check the primary text before relying on any of them. This is not legal advice.

Use the [Japan AI Regulatory Hub](01_Japan_AI_Regulatory_Hub.md) for instrument detail, and the templates in this section.

## AI practitioners

For engineers, data scientists and security staff.

- Use the guideline roles to decide who owns which risk controls.
- Record training-data sources and the copyright analysis ([JP-T2](JP-T2_Training_Data_Copyright_Record.md)).
- Apply the APPI to personal data in training and prompts.

## Product companies

For vendors, SaaS providers and platforms.

- Japanese buyers use the Guidelines for AI Business as their reference, so prepare a self-assessment ([JP-T1](JP-T1_AI_Guidelines_for_Business_Self_Assessment.md)).
- Give downstream users the information that the guidelines expect providers to share.
- Confirm the language and translation of your material for Japan.

## Auditors

For internal, external and assurance auditors.

- The criteria are soft law plus existing statutes. Say so in the report.
- Test role allocation and information sharing across the value chain.
- Review the copyright analysis of training data.

## Implementors

For programme leads, GRC teams, CISOs and DPOs.

- Adopt the guideline roles in policy.
- Add Japan to the applicability register, with APPI and copyright overlays.
- Watch for enforcement powers and for updates to the plan.

## Shared evidence list

- Guideline self-assessment
- Role allocation
- Copyright analysis
- APPI records
