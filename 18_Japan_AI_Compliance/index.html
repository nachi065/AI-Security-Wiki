    Japan AI Compliance

    Resources for people who build, sell, audit or run AI systems in Japan. The pages map Japanese laws, guidance and standards to the wiki's control library. The GCC and EU sections cover their regions in more depth and hold the general templates.

    Verification required. Everything in this section about Japanese law was compiled on 7 October 2026 from secondary web sources and from memory of the law. No primary legal text was read. Article, clause and section numbers are left blank unless a source showed them; fill them in from the primary text. Check the primary text before relying on any claim. This is not legal advice.

    Japan's position in one line

    Japan uses a promotion-and-coordination model: the AI Promotion Act (2025), a cabinet AI Basic Plan, and the non-binding Guidelines for AI Business. Binding duties come from existing law such as the APPI and copyright law [Reported].

    How claims are tagged

    Tag Meaning
    [Reported] Stated by secondary web sources found on 7 October 2026 (law-firm notes, vendor blogs, research notes). Not read in the primary text.
    [Recalled] Stated from memory of the law or guidance and not re-checked. Verify before citing it.
    [Conflict] Secondary sources disagree. Resolve from the primary text.
    [Unverified] Single source, marketing-grade source, or not researched.

    The templates are working documents, not regulator-approved forms.

    Common templates to reuse

    The GCC and EU sections contain working templates that are not tied to a region. Reuse them and adapt the regional lines.

    Need Reuse Adapt
    System intake and tiering GCC T01 or EU T01 Replace the tier names with this region's categories (section 3 of the hub)
    Inventory GCC T09 or EU T09 Add the regional columns listed in this section's templates
    Audit checklist GCC T08 or EU T08 Replace the criteria with this region's instruments
    Supplier due diligence GCC T03 or EU T03 Replace the regional questions
    Incident workflow GCC T05 or EU T05 Fill in this region's authority matrix
    Bias testing record GCC T10 Use as it is
    Risk register GCC T12 Use as it is

    Keeping this section current

    Re-check every quarter: updates to the AI Basic Plan, guideline revisions, and any enforcement or naming powers under the Act.
