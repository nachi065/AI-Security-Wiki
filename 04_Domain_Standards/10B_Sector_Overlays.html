    Sector Overlays: Financial Services, Insurance, Health and Government

    Purpose: Show what sector regulators and public-sector buyers expect of AI systems, and which wiki controls, test cases and templates meet each expectation.

    Audience: GRC and compliance teams, model risk, security architecture, vendors that sell to regulated sectors, auditors.

    Verification required. Every regional instrument named on this page comes from the regional compliance sections, which were compiled on 7 October 2026 from secondary sources and from memory of the law. No primary legal text was read. The tags [Reported], [Recalled], [Conflict] and [Unverified] are explained in each regional section. Check the primary text before relying on any of them. This is not legal advice.

    How to use this page

    Apply the wiki's controls as usual, then add the rows for your sector. Each row names the control, a test case that exercises it and the regional instruments that ask for it. The instrument IDs (for example U9 or IN5) are rows in the region's crosswalk, which gives the instrument's name and verification status. Open the regional hub for the detail.

    1. Financial services

    Banks, lenders, securities firms and payment providers.

    Expectation Wiki controls Test cases Regional instruments
    Board and senior management accountability for AI AI-CTRL-012, AI-CTRL-037 TC-L01-003, TC-L02-024 GCC: CBUAE Guidance Note (U9) [Reported]; UK: FCA approach and SM&CR (UK5) [Recalled]; India: RBI FREE-AI report (IN5) [Reported]
    Complete AI and model inventory with a risk or materiality rating AI-CTRL-011 TC-L01-001, TC-L01-005 GCC: CBUAE (U9), QCB guideline (Q4) [Reported]; Singapore: MAS guidelines, proposed (SG5) [Conflict]; UK: PRA SS1/23 (UK6) [Recalled]; Canada: OSFI E-23 (CA6) [Unverified]
    Independent validation and model risk management AI-CTRL-037, AI-CTRL-038 TC-L02-013 India: RBI draft model risk guidance (IN6) [Reported]; UK: PRA SS1/23 (UK6) [Recalled]; Canada: OSFI E-23 (CA6) [Unverified]; US: SR 11-7 (US12) [Recalled]
    Bias testing and fair customer outcomes AI-CTRL-041 TC-L03-031, TC-L03-032 GCC: CBUAE annual bias testing (U9) [Reported]; Singapore: MAS FEAT (SG6) [Recalled]; UK: Consumer Duty (UK5) [Recalled]; US: ECOA and anti-discrimination law (US12) [Recalled]
    Explanation of adverse decisions and human review AI-CTRL-041, AI-CTRL-023 TC-L03-029, TC-L03-008 GCC: CBUAE (U9) [Reported]; US: ECOA adverse action (US12) [Recalled]; EU: AI Act Art. 86 and GDPR Art. 22 [Recalled]
    Ability to stop an AI system AI-CTRL-025 TC-L01-012 GCC: CBUAE kill-switch expectation (U9) [Reported]
    Due diligence on AI suppliers and outsourcing AI-CTRL-014 TC-L03-021, TC-L02-021 GCC: CBUAE (U9) [Reported]; Australia: APRA CPS 230 (AU8) [Recalled]; EU: DORA (E6) [Reported]; India: SEBI circulars (IN7) [Reported]
    Operational resilience of services that depend on AI AI-CTRL-040 TC-L01-018 Australia: APRA CPS 230 (AU8) [Recalled]; EU: DORA (E6) [Reported]; UK: operational resilience rules (UK5) [Recalled]
    Incident reporting to the supervisor AI-CTRL-035 TC-L03-019 EU: DORA (E6) [Reported]; India: RBI, SEBI and IRDAI sector directions [Unverified]; Australia: APRA CPS 234 (AU8) [Recalled]
    Classification of credit decisions as high-risk or consequential AI-CTRL-011 TC-L03-037 EU: AI Act Annex III, essential services including credit [Reported]; US: state consequential-decision laws (US5) [Conflict]

    Templates: IN-T3 Regulated-Entity AI Readiness Checklist, UK-T2 Regulated-Firm AI Checklist, SG-T2 AI Inventory and Risk Materiality Assessment, CA-T3 OSFI E-23 Model Inventory Addendum, GCC T10 Bias Testing Record.

    SAMA expectations for Saudi Arabia were not researched.

    Expectation Wiki controls Test cases Regional instruments

    Templates: IN-T3 Regulated-Entity AI Readiness Checklist, EU T02 Impact Assessment, US-T2 Consequential-Decision and Discrimination Impact Assessment.

    4. Government and public sector

    Public bodies, and vendors that sell to them.

    Expectation Wiki controls Test cases Regional instruments
    Algorithmic impact assessment with controls set by impact level AI-CTRL-042, AI-CTRL-037 TC-L03-034, TC-L03-011 Canada: Treasury Board Directive and Algorithmic Impact Assessment (CA4) [Reported]; EU: AI Act Art. 27 for public bodies [Recalled; scope to verify]
    Published record of algorithmic tools and automated decisions AI-CTRL-013 TC-L03-036 UK: Algorithmic Transparency Recording Standard (UK11) [Recalled]; Australia: Privacy Act APP 1.7 to 1.9 (AU2) [Reported]
    Ethics self-assessment or certification asked for in tenders AI-CTRL-012, AI-CTRL-038 TC-L02-016 GCC: UAE AI Ethics self-assessment (U4) [Reported], Dubai AI Seal (U12) [Unverified]
    Government AI policy that binds public bodies AI-CTRL-012 TC-L02-001 GCC: Bahrain General Policy (B2) [Reported], Oman National AI Policy (O1) [Reported]
    Data residency and sovereignty AI-CTRL-007 TC-L03-012, TC-L03-014 GCC: see Sovereign AI, UAE Compliance and Data Residency Requirements
    Filings and contact with the authority AI-CTRL-043 TC-L03-039 China: filings with the CAC (CN1, CN2) [Reported]; South Korea: domestic representative (KR5) [Reported]

    Templates: CA-T1 Algorithmic Impact Assessment Worksheet, UK-T3 Public-Sector Algorithmic Transparency Record, AU-T1 Automated Decision Transparency Register, GCC T07 AI Ethics Self-Assessment Worksheet.
