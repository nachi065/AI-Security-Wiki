    AI Security Researcher Playbook

    Purpose: Provide role-specific guidance focused on AI threat categories, model security, AI supply chain, agentic AI, prompt injection and market research taxonomy.

    Audience: AI security researchers, threat researchers, architecture teams

    How to use: Use this page as a wiki reference. Update the evidence, owners, control status, and links as implementation maturity improves.

    Role Focus

    This playbook is focused on AI threat categories, model security, AI supply chain, agentic AI, prompt injection and market research taxonomy.

    Key Responsibilities

    • Understand applicable AI risks.
    • Identify relevant controls from the AI Security Control Objectives Library.
    • Produce evidence suitable for security review and audit.
    • Escalate gaps, exceptions and residual risks to the assigned owner.
    • Keep documentation updated when AI platforms, integrations or vendor capabilities change.

    Standard Workflow

    1. Identify the AI use case or vendor capability.
    2. Map the use case to applicable AI risks.
    3. Select required control objectives.
    4. Execute relevant PoC or validation tests.
    5. Capture evidence.
    6. Record gaps and residual risks.
    7. Submit for security, governance or audit review as applicable.

    Required Artifacts

    • AI risk review template.
    • Control mapping.
    • Evidence checklist.
    • Test results.
    • Exception log if needed.
    • Final approval or recommendation record.
